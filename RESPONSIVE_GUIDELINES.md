# Responsive guidelines

Purpose: define the approved adaptation where the supplied references do not show responsive behavior.

## Desktop and laptop

At 768 px and above, use two side-by-side panels in a centered card. The desktop target is approximately 900 × 530 px, constrained by available viewport width with safe outer padding. Keep a 300 px form column where space permits and scale spacing at narrower side-by-side widths without clipping.

Login places the form left and welcome content right. Registration reverses that relationship. Keep the diagonal division and stationary wallpaper/card shell.

## Tablet

Test both sides of the 768 px breakpoint. At the breakpoint, keep the side-by-side layout usable; below it, use the mobile stack. Do not force fixed desktop text columns into insufficient space.

## Mobile and small mobile

Below 768 px, show the active form above a compact welcome panel. Use at least 16 px input text, touch-friendly action targets, safe side padding, and vertical document scrolling. Do not force the card to fit into a short viewport or hide body overflow.

Use a short opacity transition for mode changes. Focus the incoming heading, not an input. Keep layout readable at 390 px and 320 px widths, including browser zoom and long autofilled values.

The reference establishes desktop relationships only. This stacked adaptation was explicitly selected in the approved plan.
